Maya is a junior radio engineer who works nights at a remote mountain station.
One stormy evening Maya hears a distress signal repeating the same three numbers.
She checks the equipment room and finds a damaged transmitter that should have been offline for weeks.
Maya calls Arun, the station caretaker, but Arun insists nobody has entered the equipment room.
A second signal arrives with Maya's own name hidden inside the transmission.
Maya decides to trace the signal before the storm cuts power to the station.
